## The one assumption that decides it

The board model's volumes come from Leeds's 2022 trading, not from South Yorkshire's demand, so they imply a market share. Option B applies **the same market share** by scaling A's volumes by 314,018 ÷ 685,368 = 0.458. Built-up-area population stands in for concrete demand. That is reasonable for volumetric work, which is mostly small jobs that track housing and population. It is weaker for the port and energy pipeline, but most of that work sits beside Cemex's dockside plant. If you can show Hull's demand per head is higher, use a bigger multiple and say why.
