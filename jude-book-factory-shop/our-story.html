Our story

Jude and the Book Factory

A little boy with a big hat, a big imagination and a very simple plan: get books to children.

Jude started the Book Factory when he was just four years old. He began by hiding his own books for other children to find, so that someone else could discover a story and feel the joy he felt when reading.

Jude was diagnosed with high-risk neuroblastoma. Through his treatment he kept thinking about other children, and he even designed the Book Factory logo himself. He drew himself as a Willy Wonka-style figure in a big hat, and the blue is his hearing aids.

Jude died in 2023, aged five. His family turned his project into a registered charity so that his kindness keeps reaching children, one brand new book at a time.

What we do

We give brand new children's books to hospitals, hospices, and children and families affected by grief and childhood cancer. We gift goody bags to children going through treatment, and we fund creative workshops and experiences so children can explore their own creativity.

Why we opened a shop

We buy lovely things from independent makers through the Faire wholesale marketplace, often at reduced value, and sell them on at fair prices. You get a great gift, and the profit goes straight back into books, goody bags and workshops for children who need a little magic.
