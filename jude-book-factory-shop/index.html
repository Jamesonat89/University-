Our story

It all started with one little boy and a big idea

Jude started the Book Factory when he was just four years old, with one simple goal: to get books to children. He began by hiding his own books for other children to find.

Jude was diagnosed with high-risk neuroblastoma, and died in 2023 aged five. His family turned his project into a registered charity so that his kindness keeps reaching children, one book at a time.
