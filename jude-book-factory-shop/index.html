Our story

It all started with one little boy and a big idea

Jude started the Book Factory when he was just four years old, and his idea was simple: get books to children. He began by hiding his own books for others to find, picking a book, labelling it and going into full 007 mode to create the perfect book drop.

Word spread and people wanted to join in, so Jude asked them to be his "frends". Jude died in 2023, aged five, after a diagnosis of high-risk neuroblastoma. His family are proud to carry on his mission to promote reading and make sure every child has access to books.
