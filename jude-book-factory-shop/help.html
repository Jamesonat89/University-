Delivery, returns & FAQs

How does ordering work?

Add items to your basket and place your order. We'll check everything is ready and email you a secure payment link, or you can pay when you collect. We never ask for card details by email.

Delivery & collection

Click & collect: free from our Sheffield base. We'll email to arrange a time.

UK delivery: a flat rate, free over a set amount. We aim to dispatch within 3 working days of payment.

Returns

If you change your mind you can cancel within 14 days of receiving your order and return the item unused in its original packaging. We'll refund the item price once it's back with us. Faulty or damaged? Contact us and we'll put it right. This doesn't affect your statutory rights.

Is my purchase a donation?

Shop purchases are sales rather than donations, so they can't be Gift Aided. The profit we make supports our charitable work. If you'd like to give directly, you can donate here.

Where do the products come from?

We buy from independent brands through the Faire wholesale marketplace, often at reduced value, so we can offer them below their usual retail price.

Your privacy

We use your name, contact details and address only to process and deliver your order. If you tick the updates box we'll occasionally email you about new stock and our work; you can unsubscribe any time.
